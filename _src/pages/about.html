About FXN Holdings

Always exploring.
Always building.
Always launching.

FXN Holdings is a digital venture group. We look for gaps worth filling, then build and launch the websites and platforms to fill them: online stores, travel booking, price comparison, editorial publishing and free online tools. Each one is built on modern, open-source technology to keep it lean and profitable, and each one is set up for the market it serves and the rules that apply there.

"Find the opportunity, build it well, launch it where it fits, and make it pay."

Our Story

Started with one site. Built into a portfolio.

FXN Holdings began as a single project and grew the way most good ideas do: one launch taught us how to make the next one faster, cheaper and better. Today the group runs a portfolio of platforms across e-commerce, travel, comparison, publishing and online tools. Each platform was started because we saw a real gap in a real market.

We are not tied to one product, one platform or one country. When a new opportunity appears, whether it's a growing search trend, a new partner programme, a supplier looking for reach or a region that's poorly served, we look at it, test it and, if the numbers stack up, build it.

The Operating Model

Explore. Build. Launch. Grow.

01

Explore

We research markets, search demand, competitors and partner programmes to find opportunities with real potential. Ideas get tested quickly and cheaply before they get serious investment.

02

Build

We build on the latest frameworks and proven open-source software, using our own reusable templates. That keeps build costs low, avoids vendor lock-in, and gets a platform from idea to live in weeks rather than months.

03

Launch

Every project launches for a specific region, with its legal pages, privacy controls, tax set-up and payment options matched to that market before the first visitor arrives.

04

Grow

After launch we measure everything: traffic, conversion, revenue per visitor. We improve what works, fix or drop what doesn't, and reinvest the gains so each platform reaches its targets.

What We Believe

Useful first. Profitable because of it.

  • Visitors come first.

    A platform only lasts if people find it genuinely useful, so we build tools, comparisons and content that solve a real problem.

  • Open source is an advantage.

    Mature open-source software gives us enterprise-grade foundations without enterprise-grade costs, and those savings go into the product.

  • Every market is different.

    Rules, languages, currencies and customer expectations change from one region to the next, so we build for each market rather than copying and pasting.

  • Numbers decide.

    Every project has clear targets. We keep the ones that hit them and learn from the ones that don't.

Local by Design

Different regions. Different rules. Every project built to meet them.

The group operates through entities suited to the regions we trade in. Before any platform goes live, it is set up for the privacy, consumer, advertising and tax requirements of the market it serves.

DATA

Privacy & Data Protection

Consent, cookies and personal data are handled to the law of each platform's market: GDPR and UK GDPR in Europe and the UK, and the local equivalent everywhere else.

ADS

Advertising & Consumer Law

Affiliate links and sponsored placements are clearly disclosed, and pricing, returns and consumer rights follow the rules of the country the customer buys in.

TAX

Local Entity & Tax

Each project trades through the group entity suited to its region, with VAT and sales tax registered and charged where the law requires it.
