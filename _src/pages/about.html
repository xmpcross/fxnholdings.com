About FXN Holdings

Australian owned. Built in Perth. Run for the long term.

We create and operate digital businesses. We invest carefully and run every property to the same standard.

Building digital businesses that last

FXN Holdings was set up to build and manage web properties that are genuinely useful to the people who visit them, and that earn sustainable returns over many years.

We think the best digital businesses rest on three things: quality content, technical excellence and honest monetisation. Shortcuts that trade a reader's trust for a quick commission don't survive, so we don't take them.

Founded in 2024 and based in Perth, Western Australia, we operate across five verticals: e-commerce, travel, content and publishing, price comparison, and affiliate marketing. All of them are held to the same standards of quality, compliance and performance.

  • Content strategy built around search
  • Monetisation guided by data
  • Compliance with Australian regulations
  • Design centred on the user

The principles that guide us

They shape what we build, who we partner with, and what we turn down.

Performance-driven

Every property is measured on traffic, engagement and return. We manage by the numbers, and we're honest about what they say.

Transparency

Honest content and clearly labelled monetisation underpin every business we run. Readers always know when a link earns us money.

Diversification

Working across five verticals spreads our risk, and what we learn in one market carries over to the others.

Compliance & trust

We operate under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), the Australian Privacy Principles and the Australian Consumer Law, across every property we hold.
