1. Our commitment

We aim for this website to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. Accessibility is part of how we design and build the site, not an afterthought.

2. What we have done

  • Text and interface colours meet WCAG AA contrast ratios.
  • Every page can be used with a keyboard, with visible focus and a "Skip to content" link.
  • Pages use a clear heading structure, one main heading per page, and descriptive link text.
  • Icons are decorative and hidden from screen readers; controls without visible text have accessible labels.
  • Layouts adapt to phones, tablets and desktops, and text can be zoomed to 200% without loss of content.
  • Animations respect your device's "reduce motion" setting.
  • Form fields have labels, and errors are announced and explained.

3. Known limitations

Some third-party websites we link to, including our own portfolio websites, are run separately and may not meet the same standard. The AI website assistant produces automated replies that may not always be perfectly worded for every assistive technology; you can always use the contact form instead.

4. Feedback and help

If you have trouble using any part of this website, or need information in another format, email contact@fxnholdings.com with "Accessibility" in the subject line. Tell us the page and the problem, and we will get back to you.

5. Review of this statement

We review this statement when we make significant changes to the website. The date above shows the latest revision.
