1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. Similar technologies include local storage and pixels.

2. The cookies we use

This Website uses only strictly necessary cookies and storage — those essential for it to function and be secure. We do not use analytics, performance, advertising or social-media tracking cookies, and we do not build profiles about visitors.

When you close the cookie notice, we remember that choice in your browser's local storage so the notice doesn't appear again. If you use the website assistant, your current conversation is kept in your browser's session storage so it stays visible as you move between pages. It is deleted when you close the tab, is not used for tracking and is not shared.

Where cookies or similar storage involve personal information, we handle it in line with our Privacy Policy and the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

If we later add analytics or marketing tools, we will add a consent banner and update this policy.

3. Managing cookies

Most browsers let you view, manage, delete and block cookies. Because we use only strictly necessary storage, blocking it may stop parts of the Website from working. Guidance is available at www.aboutcookies.org or in your browser’s help section.

4. Changes to this Cookie Policy

If our use of cookies changes, we will update this page and, where required by law, ask for your consent.
