How we work

The same three steps for every platform we build.

01

Explore opportunities

We look for markets with steady demand and a clear gap: products, prices or information that people struggle to find in one place.

02

Build on open source

We build on proven open-source frameworks and our own reusable templates, so a new platform needs less custom work and costs less to run.

03

Launch locally

Each platform launches for a specific market, set up for its language, currency, privacy rules and consumer law from day one.

Latest frameworks. Open-source foundations.

Open-source software and reusable templates mean we don't start each platform from scratch. Proven building blocks let every new platform reach its targets sooner, and improvements to a template carry over to every platform built on it.
