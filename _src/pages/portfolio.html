Our verticals

What each part of the group does, and how it earns.

01

E-commerce

Online retail, built to convert.

Specialist online stores plus coupon and discount sites, including furfunshop.com, nxt.deals and nxtdiscount.com. Each one is built on proven commerce technology, structured for search, and designed for buying on a phone.

  • Category-led catalogues
  • Mobile-first design
  • Search-optimised product pages
  • Coupons & discount codes
  • Clear pricing
  • Australian Consumer Law compliance
How it earns

Direct sales, retail partnerships and affiliate commission on coupon and deal sites.

02

Travel & booking

Inspiring the trip, then helping book it.

Travel properties that help people plan trips, from destination guides to flight and hotel comparison. Bookings go through established travel and affiliate partners, so travellers get live prices and availability.

  • Destination guides
  • Hotel comparison
  • Flight search
  • Holiday packages
  • Itinerary planning
  • Partner booking
How it earns

Commission from travel and booking partners.

03

Content & publishing

Useful writing that earns its rankings.

Editorial publications covering lifestyle, home, technology, beauty and travel. Each is written to answer real questions clearly and organised in topic clusters so readers, and search engines, can find their way around.

  • Researched, fact-checked articles
  • Long-form guides
  • Topic clusters
  • Newsletters
  • Social distribution
  • Labelled sponsored content
How it earns

Advertising, sponsorship and affiliate partnerships.

04

Price comparison & deals

Helping people decide with confidence.

Comparison platforms that bring together product and service prices from many retailers. They show clear, up-to-date price data so consumers can see the real difference before they buy.

  • Multi-retailer comparison
  • Regular price updates
  • Deal discovery
  • Clear comparison tables
  • Product specs
  • Independent rankings
How it earns

Referral commission from retailers, disclosed on every page.

05

Free online tools

Built to solve one job well.

Practical tools for marketers, site owners and everyday users. They're free to use, fast to load, and built to solve one job well, like the browser-based SEO tools on fxnseo.com.

  • Browser-based
  • No sign-up
  • Fast to load
  • One job, done well
