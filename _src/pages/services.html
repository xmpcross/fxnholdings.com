01

Opportunity Research

Before we build anything, we work out whether it's worth building. We look at search demand, competition, partner programmes and margins to find the markets where a new platform can win.

  • Keyword, search and market demand research
  • Competitor and gap analysis by region
  • Partner, supplier and affiliate programme review
  • Quick, low-cost tests before full build
02

Platform Build & Launch

We design and build on current frameworks and proven open-source software, using our own reusable templates, so each new platform launches faster and costs less to run.

  • Modern front ends with Next.js, React and Tailwind CSS
  • Headless content and product data with Strapi
  • Commerce on Shopify and WooCommerce with supplier integrations
  • Workflow automation with n8n; containerised hosting on Docker, Hetzner and Vercel
03

Local Setup & Compliance

Every launch is set up for its market. Legal pages, consent, disclosures, tax and payments are matched to the region before the platform goes live.

  • Privacy and cookie consent to local law (GDPR, UK GDPR and equivalents)
  • Clear affiliate and advertising disclosures
  • VAT and sales-tax set-up through the right group entity
  • Local currency, language and payment options where they matter
04

Growth & Monetisation

Once a platform is live, we grow it with search, content and data, and earn from it in ways that don't get in the visitor's way.

  • SEO, structured data and AI-search readiness
  • Affiliate, advertising and direct product revenue
  • Analytics-led testing and conversion improvements
  • Shared learnings and infrastructure across the portfolio
