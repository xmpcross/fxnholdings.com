1. Who we are

This website, www.fxnholdings.com (the “Website”), is operated by FXN Holdings (“we”, “us”, “our”), an Australian business (ABN 53 274 423 748).

Postal address: PO Box 500, WEST PERTH WA 6872. Contact email: kritin@fxnholdings.com.

FXN Holdings is responsible for the personal information collected through the Website under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

2. The personal data we collect

This is an informational corporate website. It does not sell products or services, require an account, or use analytics or advertising tracking. We keep data collection to a minimum.

  • Information you give us: when you contact us by email or through a form, we receive your name, email address, company (if provided) and the contents of your message.
  • Technical data collected automatically: our hosting provider and web server may log limited technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, referring page, and the date and time of your visit.
  • Cookies and storage: we use only strictly necessary storage — see our Cookie Policy.

3. Why we use your information

We collect and use personal information only for the purposes below, or for related purposes you would reasonably expect. We do not use automated decision-making or profiling, and we do not knowingly collect sensitive information.

Purpose Why we need it
Responding to your enquiries and correspondence To reply to you and deal with what you have asked for
Operating, maintaining and securing the Website To keep the Website running and secure
Complying with legal obligations Where an Australian law requires it

4. How we share your data

We do not sell your personal data and do not share it for advertising purposes. We may disclose data to:

  • service providers who help us run the Website (such as our hosting and email providers);
  • professional advisers (such as lawyers or accountants) where necessary;
  • public authorities or regulators where required by law.

5. Overseas disclosure

Some of our service providers (such as our hosting and email providers) may store or process personal information outside Australia. Where we disclose personal information overseas, we take reasonable steps to ensure it is handled consistently with the Australian Privacy Principles.

6. How long we keep your data

We may keep certain information longer where required to comply with a legal obligation or to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

  • Enquiry and correspondence data: kept only as long as needed to handle your enquiry and a reasonable period afterwards, then deleted.
  • Server-log data: kept for a short period for security and diagnostics, then deleted or anonymised.

7. Your rights

Under the Australian Privacy Principles you can ask to access the personal information we hold about you and ask us to correct it. You can also ask us to delete it or to stop contacting you.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at kritin@fxnholdings.com. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Complaints

If you believe we have not handled your data properly, please contact us first. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can lodge a complaint with:

  • Australia: Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC), GPO Box 5288, Sydney NSW 2001 — oaic.gov.au
  • If you live outside Australia, you can also contact the data-protection authority in your country of residence.

9. Visitors from outside Australia

  • European Economic Area: we process EEA visitors’ data consistently with the EU GDPR (Regulation (EU) 2016/679).
  • California, USA: we do not sell or share your personal information as defined under the CCPA/CPRA; California residents may exercise rights to know, delete and correct by contacting us.
  • United Kingdom: we process UK visitors’ data consistently with the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.
  • Other countries: we comply with applicable data-protection laws where we operate.

10. Children

The Website is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date above shows the latest revision; material changes will be posted on this page.
