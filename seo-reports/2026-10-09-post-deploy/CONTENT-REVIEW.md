Local source review covered all five posts. No website edits made. Corporate digital venture group; ecommerce product/local storefront requirements do not apply.

## Priority findings

### High: correct material tax-guide qualifications before promoting these guides

[UK VAT article](https://fxnholdings.com/insights/uk-vat-for-online-sellers-when-to-register/) says overseas sellers holding UK marketplace stock must maintain VAT registration. It omits the exemption available to sellers making only zero-rated deemed supplies. [HMRC marketplace guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/vat-and-overseas-goods-sold-to-customers-in-the-uk-using-online-marketplaces) explicitly lists registration OR applying for exemption in this scenario; [HMRC registration manual](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/vat-registration-manual/vatreg37210) confirms this. Correct the universal statement and distinguish registration liability, approved exemption, and import-VAT recovery. Its general £90,000 threshold is supported by current HMRC guidance; this is not a finding that the threshold itself is wrong.

[US business article](https://fxnholdings.com/insights/starting-a-us-business-from-overseas/) tells applicants without US tax credentials to enter Foreign/N/A on SS-4 line 7b. [IRS instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/iss4) specify the responsible party must both lack AND be ineligible for an SSN/ITIN. Add this condition. The same article states foreign registered entities must file BOI without mentioning applicable exemptions, and links to an older compliance PDF. [Current FinCEN guidance](https://www.fincen.gov/boi) limits the rule to foreign reporting companies not qualifying for exemption and announces a final rule effective 14 August 2026. Link to current guidance, preserve the correct US-created-entity exemption, and qualify the foreign reporting obligation.

These are editorial correctness findings on consequential content, not proof of a search penalty. Have a qualified reviewer validate the complete articles; this is a spot check, not comprehensive legal/tax advice.

### High: qualified review is not identifiable for consequential guides

Both live guides show FXN Holdings as byline and Organization as schema author; there is no named qualified reviewer or linked credentials. Their source lists, publication dates and general-information disclaimers are useful, but do not establish who verified the advice. Add a genuine author/reviewer biography, relevant credentials, review date and correction mechanism after review occurs. Do not invent credentials or replace valid Organization authorship merely for schema scoring. [About](https://fxnholdings.com/about/) explains the operating model but does not identify the reviewing people.

### Medium: demonstrate first-hand experience with portfolio case studies

[Portfolio](https://fxnholdings.com/portfolio/) lists 14 actual project domains and their stacks, a useful existing trust signal. [About](https://fxnholdings.com/about/) and [Services](https://fxnholdings.com/services/) describe speed, efficiency and market compliance broadly without dated case studies or demonstrable results. Add one or two substantiated project stories: initial market gap, launch constraints, work performed, measured outcomes with period/method, and limitations. No fabricated revenue/traffic figures. Link stories contextually from market-gap and AI posts. This improves partner due diligence and gives AI systems original, attributable facts; it is an opportunity, not a minimum-word-count requirement.

### Medium: brand ambiguity warrants clearer entity signals

A fresh web search for the exact brand also returned First Trust Energy AlphaDEX Fund holdings and a UK FXN Holdings Limited registration. These are not evidence that either entity is this site. Keep consistent Australian ABN, Perth identity, venture-group descriptor and verified entity links across home/About/legal/schema and legitimate owned profiles. Existing footer ABN and Organization sameAs link to the ABR are positives. Do not create Wikipedia pages or claim third-party authority without evidence. Search sampling does not establish Google ranking positions or index coverage.

### Low/opportunity: deepen helpful topic pathways rather than expand indiscriminately

The blog has five posts across four categories: company news (1), technology/AI (1), market gaps (1), starting a business (2). Existing article templates have TOCs, category breadcrumbs, related-post cards and contextual corporate links. No blanket thin-content defect is assigned to short company updates. Most promising new coverage is first-party launch case studies, architecture tradeoffs, and explicit human-review workflow examples. The tax cluster needs qualified review before expansion. These are intent-based editorial groupings, not measured SERP-overlap clusters; no search volumes, cannibalization claims or 30-keyword expansion are claimed.

## GEO and SXO observations

- Important content is present in delivered HTML; it is not dependent on running JavaScript for extraction.
- Live robots.txt is `User-agent: * / Allow: /` with the sitemap URL. No explicit robots ban on search/AI crawlers. This does not prove Cloudflare permits every bot or guarantee AI citations.
- `/llms.txt` returns 404. Optional experimental opportunity only; not a Google indexing/ranking requirement and not a priority fix.
- Tax guides already use question headings, immediate answer paragraphs, TOCs, dates, lists and primary-source links. Their extractability makes correcting oversimplified statements especially important.
- Do not enforce an alleged ideal 134–167-word AI passage, keyword density target, or blanket page word-count floor. These are not established platform requirements.
- Corporate home and portfolio match navigational/partner-evaluation intent. A supplier can identify portfolio and contact paths. A prospective founder can see capabilities but would benefit from clearer engagement criteria and an example collaboration. A tax-information seeker gets an appropriately structured guide but insufficient visible reviewer expertise.
- Search sample for VAT topics surfaced official HMRC guidance and its manuals, supporting informational-guide intent. US topic surfaced SBA and SelectUSA business-structure resources. Only limited result snippets were inspected; no complete five-page competitive SXO study, localized Google top-ten consensus, keyword-volume estimate or numerical SXO score is claimed.
